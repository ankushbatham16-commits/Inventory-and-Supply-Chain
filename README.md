# 📦 Inventory & Supply Chain Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Inventory & Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard** built with **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze inventory performance, warehouse utilization, transportation costs, lead time, backorders, units sold, and regional/category-level performance.

The dashboard converts supply chain data into meaningful **KPIs and business insights** to help understand inventory efficiency and operational performance.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Inventory & Supply Chain Report** provides a consolidated view of key supply chain metrics through interactive visualizations and filters.

The dashboard focuses on:

* Warehouse utilization
* Inventory turnover
* Days Sales of Inventory
* Transportation cost
* Units sold
* Lead time
* Backorders
* Inventory levels
* Regional performance
* Category performance
* Year-wise trends

---

## 🎯 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks three major inventory KPIs:

### 🏭 Warehouse Utilization

Measures how efficiently available warehouse capacity is being utilized.

### 📦 Inventory Turnover Ratio

Shows how efficiently inventory is being sold and replaced during a period.

### 📅 Days Sales of Inventory

Measures the approximate number of days inventory remains before being sold.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 🏭 Warehouse Utilization

A dedicated KPI and gauge visualization is used to monitor warehouse utilization and understand warehouse capacity performance.

### 🚚 Transportation Cost Analysis

Transportation costs are analyzed by:

* Region
* Category

This helps identify areas where logistics costs may be higher.

### 📦 Units Sold Trend

A line chart analyzes **units sold over time**, allowing users to identify changes and trends in sales activity.

### ⏱️ Lead Time Analysis

Lead time is analyzed across product categories to identify categories with longer supply cycles.

### ⚠️ Backorder Analysis

Backorders are analyzed based on **Order Status** to understand the distribution of pending or affected orders.

### 🏪 Inventory Level Analysis

Inventory levels are compared across:

* Categories
* Regions

This helps identify areas with potentially high or low inventory levels.
